# Session 2026-04-12

- Scope: Reduce octilinear earth-guide line detail by about 20%.

- Topic: Octilinear preview roughness
  - Decision: Increase the default Douglas-Peucker tolerance in `build_octilinear_earth_guide_svg.py` from `1.8` to `2.16`.
  - Rationale: The linework was still too busy in a few regions, and the user asked for roughly 20 percent more roughness. Raising the simplification tolerance keeps the same rendering pipeline and polar handling while reducing coastline detail enough to make the large-scale landmass shape easier to read.

- Topic: Island preservation while roughening
  - Decision: Cap simplification at `1.8` for rings under `80 deg^2` before applying the broader `2.16` default.
  - Rationale: The global roughening made Japan disappear because a medium-sized island ring was simplified too aggressively. Keeping smaller rings closer to the previous tolerance preserves recognizable archipelagos while still roughening the large continental outlines that dominate the guide.

- Topic: Small-island detail recovery
  - Decision: Use three area buckets for simplification: `2.16` for large continental rings, `1.8` for medium island rings, `1.4` for smaller islands, and `1.0` for tiny island rings.
  - Rationale: The UK still vanished under the two-tier cap because some island rings were being flattened too much. A gentler gradient keeps the continental simplification roughly the same while restoring enough shape detail for islands to remain recognizable.

- Topic: Slightly finer overall mesh
  - Decision: Lower the default simplification tolerance from `2.16` to `2.0` while keeping the island-preserving area buckets.
  - Rationale: The user wanted a modest increase in mesh detail, not a new rendering strategy. Reducing only the global baseline preserves the earlier island fixes and makes the entire outline a little less coarse without undoing the octilinear simplification work.

- Topic: Tiny-island recovery
  - Decision: Lower the tiny-island simplification ceiling from `1.0` to `0.8`.
  - Rationale: Taiwan-sized rings were still being simplified away at `1.0`. Dropping the ceiling a bit more restores the smallest visible islands without changing the coarser treatment for larger landmasses.

- Topic: Taiwan area filtering
  - Decision: Base the ring-area filter on the raw polygon area and make it adaptive by size bucket, with island-sized rings allowed down to `2 deg^2`.
  - Rationale: Taiwan was being dropped by the post-simplification area filter even when the shape itself still had enough geometry to be recognizable. Filtering on the raw ring area keeps the island from disappearing while still suppressing tiny noise rings.
