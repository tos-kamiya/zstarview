- Topic: Star interpolation Gate 1 measurement
  - Decision: Mark the current single-homography approach Not Go under a
    1-pixel maximum-error budget and proceed to the mesh-warp implementation.
  - Evidence: In a 1600x900, 90-degree field with the circular sky-disc area
    sampled at 25x15 points and elapsed times of +/-30 seconds, north-facing
    views reached about 0.59 px maximum error, while south, zenith, and
    low-altitude views reached about 10.5 to 15.8 px.
  - Rationale: A single global homography is sufficiently accurate only for
    some orientations and cannot represent the position-dependent sky motion
    consistently across representative views.

- Topic: Star interpolation Gate 2 mesh experiment
  - Decision: Mark the interior-cell 16x9 mesh result Go under the 1-pixel
    maximum-error budget, while keeping circular sky-disc boundary clipping as
    a separate pending rendering check.
  - Evidence: `dev-samples/measure_star_mesh_gate.py` measured a maximum error

  - Rationale: Piecewise-linear interpolation between correctly reprojected

    circular clip boundary.

- Topic: Gate measurement interpretation and next implementation scope
  - Decision: Use 1600x900 as the stress-test surface and define the mesh by a
    square pixel cell size rather than by a fixed rows/columns pair. The
    100px baseline therefore produces a 16x9 mesh.
  - Measurement procedure: For each mesh phase `(0.0, 0.0)`, `(0.5, 0.0)`,
    `(0.0, 0.5)`, and `(0.5, 0.5)`, compare triangle-interpolated positions
    against direct 3D sidereal rotation plus screen reprojection. Test the
    north, south, zenith, and low-altitude view directions at `-30s` and
    `+30s`.
  - Results: The square 16x9 mesh produced a worst interior-cell error of
    approximately `0.0158 px`, well below the provisional `1.0 px` Gate 2
    threshold. Earlier reference measurements showed that the single global
    3x3 homography reached approximately `0.59 px` in the north-facing case
    and `10.5` to `15.8 px` in the other representative orientations.
  - Boundary: Cells whose four corners are not inside the circular sky disc
    are excluded from this numerical gate. The transparent surface boundary,
    clipping, triangle seams, and actual QImage composition remain a separate
    rendering validation item.
  - Implementation consequence: Proceed with square-pixel mesh deformation
    in the renderer, retain the 3D rotation/reprojection path as the reference
    oracle, and keep the 1px gate for later visual and numerical validation.

- Topic: Move mesh preparation out of normal paint work
  - Decision: Prepare the star interpolation mesh during the even-second
    dynamic display tick and cache it by viewport, view, interval, snapshot,
    and display-time inputs. Normal paint operations reuse the cached vertices
    and targets; a cache miss retains a safe fallback calculation.
  - Rationale: The mesh path performs per-cell affine solves, clip-path setup,
    and image draws on the GUI thread. Moving vertex preparation out of paint
    removes repeated NumPy geometry work from mouse and repaint handling while
    preserving the existing GUI interaction mode.
  - Validation: Relevant GUI/render tests passed (77 tests); targeted `ruff`
    and `git diff --check` passed.

- Topic: Avoid a full viewport surface for twinkle
  - Decision: Transform only the screen positions of the transient twinkle

  - Rationale: At most 30 twinkle targets are active, so allocating and
    rasterizing a full viewport-sized transparent `QImage` was unnecessary

  - Validation: 46 targeted tests passed with `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`;

- Topic: Avoid a full viewport surface for simplified star labels
  - Decision: Transform each named star's label anchor through the mesh and

  - Rationale: Named-star labels are sparse, and the text should remain sharp

- Topic: Remove mesh overscan freeze outside the sky disc
  - Decision: Let all rectangular viewport mesh vertices follow the projected
    sidereal motion, including vertices in the transparent area outside the
    circular sky disc.
  - Rationale: Freezing the outside vertices created a discontinuity at the
    disc boundary and was observed as flicker in semi-transparent pixels.

- Topic: Scheduler probe compatibility with optional star mesh preparation
  - Decision: Treat star interpolation mesh preparation as an optional render
    hook when publishing a dynamic display tick.
  - Rationale: The production window provides the hook through its render
    mixin, while lightweight scheduler probes intentionally compose only the
    update mixin. A missing cache-preparation hook can safely use the existing
    render-time fallback and must not prevent dynamic-layer publication.
  - Validation: `tests/test_scheduler_idle.py` passed (8 tests); the full
