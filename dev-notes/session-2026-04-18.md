# Session 2026-04-18

- Topic: Satellite hover styling
  - Decision: Keep satellite hover independent from star hover, show the satellite name in the satellite overlay color, and emphasize the hovered satellite by thickening the cross marker instead of drawing a circle.
  - Rationale: The circle-style hover cue is visually tied to stars and makes satellites feel like a second-class target. A thicker cross preserves the satellite glyph identity while still giving a clear hover affordance, and independent hover resolution keeps satellite discovery reliable near named stars.
