## Star-surface temporal interpolation review

- Topic: Reduce the visible step when the 60-second sky snapshot is replaced
  - Decision: Do not implement yet; treat a center-based 2D matrix as a useful
    prototype but not as the final motion model for a large or wide-FOV window.
  - Rationale: The current viewer replaces a cached base frame after a
    background sky snapshot completes. A single planar transform can approximate
    the short-term motion near the view center, but Earth rotation produces a
    position-dependent alt/az motion after the spherical projection. Large FOVs,
    the horizon, and the zenith make the approximation visibly non-uniform.
  - Follow-up: If pursued, prefer a lightweight per-frame star-only reprojection
    from inertial direction vectors, while keeping the expensive sky snapshot at
    its existing cadence. Evaluate a 100-250 ms animation repaint separately
    from the snapshot interval; a 10-second repaint cadence may remain stepped.

- Topic: Document the star-surface interpolation proposal
  - Decision: Update the user-facing specification and internal rendering/cache
    design with the unimplemented split between dark-star surface rendering and
    direct bright-star compositing.
  - Rationale: The proposal must preserve the semi-transparent dark underlay's
    `SourceOver` behavior while keeping bright stars and diamonds crisp; a single
    additive image would not darken the sky behind bright stars.
- Result: Documented 60-second sky snapshots, 3x3 transform sampling across
    the viewport including overscan corners, separate cache responsibilities,
    and repaint/cache-key requirements. No source code was changed.

- Topic: First implementation of star interpolation
  - Decision: Introduce the feature incrementally. Separate the dark-star
    surface and bright-star direct pass first, then connect a 9-point
    3x3 homography approximation to the regular scenic viewer in 10-second
    cache buckets.
  - Rationale: This makes the composition modes and alpha behavior testable
    before optimizing away all base-frame work. Atlas remains on its existing
    path during the trial.
  - Verification: Related tests passed (`120 passed`), ruff passed, and the
    full suite reached `1277 passed, 1 failed`; excluding the existing-looking
    overscan expectation mismatch leaves `1266 passed`.
