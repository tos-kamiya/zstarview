# Session 2026-05-13

- Topic: Water overlay alpha simplification
  - Decision: Use `water_surface_opacity` directly as the water dot alpha and remove the extra internal multiplier.
  - Rationale: The previous constant-based scaling made the effective opacity harder to reason about and conflicted with the CLI option name. The new default is reduced to about one quarter of the previous visible strength.
  - Notes: Default water opacity was updated to `0.12` across CLI, GUI, render defaults, and docs.
