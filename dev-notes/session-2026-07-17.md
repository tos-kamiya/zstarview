# Session 2026-07-17

Scope: Reorganize the sky-disc color-model section from the implementation.

- Topic: Document sky-disc color factors in implementation order
  - Decision: Rewrite `docs/design/rendering-pipeline.md` section 2.1 as a factor-by-factor description covering the night cutoff, altitude base color, haze, solar-angle blue, solar-altitude blue, anti-solar shading, solar glow, sunset, twilight, and post-processing.
  - Rationale: The previous text described sunset as an appended concept and did not show how it relates to the other factors. The new structure follows `_get_sky_color_vectorized` and distinguishes hue-distribution factors from final intensity/alpha controls.

- Topic: Reduce warm sunset tint in the solar glare
  - Decision: Add a direction-dependent sunset factor that reduces the sunset contribution to one third at the exact solar direction while leaving the horizon-side contribution unchanged.
  - Rationale: The existing sunset term already depends on `forward`, so scaling that term only at `forward = 1` reduces the warm solar glare without globally weakening the sunset correction.

- Topic: Reduce sky-disc haze strength
  - Decision: Halve `HAZE_STRENGTH_MIN` from `0.16` to `0.08` and `HAZE_STRENGTH_MAX` from `0.68` to `0.34`.
  - Rationale: Reduce the `HAZE_RGB` blend directly without introducing another tuning parameter or changing the rest of the sky-color model.

- Topic: Shift haze and sunset colors
  - Decision: Move `HAZE_RGB` slightly toward blue (`0.70, 0.70, 0.67` to `0.68, 0.70, 0.72`) and move `SUNSET_RGB` toward red (`1.00, 0.54, 0.20` to `1.00, 0.48, 0.14`).
  - Rationale: Add a restrained cool cast to the reduced haze while making the sunset correction more distinctly red-orange without changing its spatial or altitude behavior.

- Topic: Increase separation between high-sun blue and low-sun red
  - Decision: Increase `SUN_ALT_BLUE_STRENGTH` from `0.02` to `0.10` and `SUNSET_STRENGTH` from `0.21` to `0.30`.
  - Rationale: The supplied screenshots showed the high-sun sky remaining too gray and the low-sun sky insufficiently warm at `sky opacity = 0.5`. Strengthening the existing altitude-specific factors increases the contrast without adding a new branch or parameter.
