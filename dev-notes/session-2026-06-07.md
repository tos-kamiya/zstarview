# Session 2026-06-07

- Scope: Investigate why `zstarview-export-image "@34.68704549281618, 135.5244422242144" -A5 --search "C/1861 G1" -D-21 -H5 -o tmp.png` can show water above the ground fill.
- Topic: Terrain horizon resolution in export-image
  - Decision: Increase the export-image terrain horizon sampling density to 0.5 degree azimuth steps and 60 m distance samples.
  - Rationale: The previous 1.0 degree / 90 m scan could miss a higher ridge in a narrow azimuth sector, which made the water overlay appear above the main horizon in some directions.
- Topic: Export-image water path parity with GUI
  - Decision: Stop feeding a fresh DEM sampler into export-image water sampling so its inland-water heights follow the same GUI path.
  - Rationale: GUI `WaterOverlayController` uses the terrain-controller ground height already present in `ViewerData` and does not refit inland water against a new DEM during water overlay generation. Export-image had been doing that extra DEM-based height correction, which produced a different water surface and made the overlay appear above the horizon in cases where GUI did not.
