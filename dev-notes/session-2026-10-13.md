Scope: Make cache history retention a persistent, bounded user setting.

- Topic: Configurable cache history retention
  - Decision: Store one retention-hours setting in `config.json`, expose it in
    the startup dialog's General tab, and apply it to cloud, aircraft,
    satellite, Geo-satellite, and `--place` launch history cleanup. Use a
    24-hour default and clamp values to 1 through 720 hours.
  - Rationale: One saved value keeps the related history stores aligned while
    preserving separate live freshness and stale-fallback behavior.

Session summary: Added a saved cache retention setting with a 1-hour minimum,

Tests were not run.
