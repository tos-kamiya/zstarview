- Topic: Align Road Lights performance limiting with urban outlines
  - Decision: Define `--road-light-max-candidates` with a default of `5000`, applied per OSM road `way` before simplification, DEM sampling, and projection. Keep the full fetched geometry in cache, and select candidates by visual-priority scoring rather than retrieval order.
  - Rationale: Road Lights can produce both a continuous stroke and lamp points from one way, so way-level accounting gives a stable and understandable limit while preserving both visual components. Keeping the cache uncapped allows changes in view or limit to reuse existing data.

- Topic: Road Lights status-line contents
  - Decision: Do not show candidate or selected counts in the normal status line, matching urban-outline status behavior. Candidate counts may be logged for diagnostics.
  - Rationale: Status lines should remain short and communicate source/availability rather than internal performance metrics.

- Topic: Road candidate ranking refinement
  - Decision: Rank candidate `way` objects only by their minimum observer distance, with a stable identifier as the deterministic tie-breaker. Do not use angular length, vertex count, highway class, retrieval order, or view direction.
  - Rationale: Road height is treated as constant, and angular length depends on how the source data splits a physical road into ways. Distance-only ranking avoids making that storage detail part of the visual selection policy.
