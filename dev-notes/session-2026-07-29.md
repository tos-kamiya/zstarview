- Topic: Replace daytime sky color tuning with an atmospheric path model
  - Decision: Evaluate a spherical-Earth, parallel-sunlight, single-scattering model before changing the renderer. For each viewing ray, integrate sunlight scattered toward the observer through the atmosphere up to 500 km, while excluding direct solar radiance.
  - Rationale: The current sky renderer combines many empirical altitude, solar-angle, sunset, glow, and anti-solar corrections. A path integral gives those changes a common geometric and spectral cause. Attenuation alone cannot produce visible sky radiance, so the model must include a scattering term; this remains consistent with excluding direct sunlight.
  - Open Point: Decide the atmospheric density profile, RGB extinction/scattering coefficients, solar spectrum, and display exposure as explicit physical inputs rather than independent visual correction terms.
