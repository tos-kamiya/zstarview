  - Decision: Raise the maximum radial jitter from 1.25 px to 5.0 px as a temporary visual check.

- Topic: Express water-surface radial jitter in meters
  - Decision: Treat the 5.0 maximum offset as meters of scan-radius displacement and scale it to screen coordinates using each ring's projected radius per ground meter.
  - Rationale: Keep the displacement observer-radial and make its magnitude independent of display resolution.

- Topic: Correct ground-distance projection for water zigzags
  - Decision: Remove the screen-radius scale conversion. Recover the surface radius from the original sight line using a spherical Earth approximation, change ground distance by the meter offset, and project the resulting altitude at the original azimuth.
  - Rationale: The screen center is the view direction, not the observer's ground position. Dividing screen radius by scan distance exaggerated displacement and produced long radial streaks in the supplied screenshot.
  - Validation: Reviewed the projection code and checked patch whitespace. Tests were not changed or run for this visual iteration.

  - Decision: Cap the radial offset at 20% of the nearest scan-ring interval while retaining the 5 m maximum.
  - Rationale: Near-observer rings have very small meter spacing, so a fixed 5 m offset can cross many adjacent rings and create dense streaks.
