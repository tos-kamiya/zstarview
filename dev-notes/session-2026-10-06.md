- Topic: Correct ground-distance projection for water zigzags
  - Decision: Remove the screen-radius scale conversion. Recover the surface radius from the original sight line using a spherical Earth approximation, change ground distance by the meter offset, and project the resulting altitude at the original azimuth.
  - Rationale: The screen center is the view direction, not the observer's ground position. Dividing screen radius by scan distance exaggerated displacement and produced long radial streaks in the supplied screenshot.
  - Validation: Reviewed the projection code and checked patch whitespace. Tests were not changed or run for this visual iteration.
