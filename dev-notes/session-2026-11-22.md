- Topic: Night lights on the far side of the Earth
  - Decision: Recommend evaluating a separate reduced-resolution global raster for the Earth guide while retaining on-demand high-resolution tiles for local glow; implementation and distribution are not yet decided.
  - Rationale: The current eight 15-arcsecond tiles serve a 128 km local footprint and are large; global display needs a far coarser representation, aggregation that preserves small cities, and solar altitude evaluated at each surface point.
  - Alternatives Considered: Downloading every full-resolution tile on first launch simplifies reuse but increases initial transfer and storage substantially; optional prefetch remains useful for travel or offline use.

- Topic: Prototype 0.1-degree global night lights
  - Decision: Generate a float32 mean-radiance GeoTIFF and a log-scaled grayscale preview from all eight verified 2025 VIIRS tiles.
  - Rationale: Preserve the averaged values separately from the adjustable display mapping; include zero-valued source pixels in the mean because nodata metadata otherwise causes overestimated brightness. Source zeros also include missing data and cannot be distinguished here.
  - Validation: Produced a 3600 x 1800 global raster (2,872,176 bytes) and PNG (600,480 bytes), checked a Tokyo cell against the original 24 x 24 pixels, inspected the image, and passed Ruff. The dev-samples directory is git-ignored.

  - Validation: Verified input tile checksums, global bounds and resolution, and a Tokyo cell against its 12 x 12 source pixels; Ruff passed. The GeoTIFF is 8,273,860 bytes and the PNG is 1,911,272 bytes. Missing temporary input tiles were downloaded again after reboot.
